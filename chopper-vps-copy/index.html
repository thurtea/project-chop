The game

You start beside a ruined hut. Repair it, gather wood, and build floors, walls, roofs, stairs, torches, and campfires. Fish the ponds and the sea. Mine the forests. Fight through crypts for gear.

The world is four forests: Water, Earth, Fire, and Wind. Each world grows from a seed, so friends can share the same map. Trees you cut grow back. At night the woods come after you. Stay close to home and the night stays quiet.

One to four players. The host shares a six-character code, or invites Steam friends. Each player keeps their own gear. The host's world saves at dawn.

One developer. Built in Unity.
