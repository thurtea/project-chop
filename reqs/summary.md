Last updated: 2026-10-06 (v0.2.9: the house works, building pieces snap corner to corner, mining is a pastime like fishing, the character creator is back, and the axe no longer flips while running). The playtest exe at `Builds/Release/windows/Chopper.exe` is still stamped 4:01 PM and does not have the afternoon's mining or snapping; rebuild it once the editor is closed. The old long version is in `Docs/archive/2026-10-02/summary.md`. The design plan (historical) is `Docs/CHOPPER_PLAN.md`; the road to the Steam demo and 1.0 is `Docs/STATUS.md`; what to buy or find is `requirements.md`.

Style rule for everything (docs, code comments, commits, in-game text): no emojis and no em dashes.

## The game in one paragraph

A co-op survival game in Unity 6 (URP, FishNet networking). Chop trees by day; felling a forest tree plants saplings (hub trees plant none, so staying home keeps the night quiet). At night the saplings wake up as tree enemies and attack you, anything you built, and the hut (a walk-in house, no palisade since 2026-10-05). Sleeping in the bed once every player is in it skips the night. A broken hut is a ruin (caved roof, knocked down walls, planks, cold fireplace: the RuinOnly parts) and repairing swaps in the whole house (IntactOnly, Hut.ApplyRuinLook). Yard torches are the held torch model scaled up (CharacterSetup.BuildTorchModel). View distance: exponential squared fog per zone (ZonePalette.FogDensity, hub 0.0045, half fog at about 185 m) and a 600 m camera far clip (ZonePalette.FarClip). The hut sits in a safe hub with four elemental forests around it (north Water, east Earth, south Fire, west Wind). Killing an elemental enemy charges your axe with that element for 15 hits, which does bonus damage to the element it beats. Fell too many trees in one grove and its warden, a huge tree boss, rises there.

## What works now

- **The world is generated from a seed.**
  - New Game shows a seed (random, or type any number or word).
  - Hills, the hub pond, marsh pools, a deep lake in each of the Fire, Earth and Wind forests, paths, rocks, trees, the grass map and the wake up spots all come from that seed, built at runtime (`World/Generation`).
  - The layout stays the same in every world: hut on its mound in the middle, four forests around it, beach and sea at the edge. Steep mountains ring the hub, with one narrow gorge on each path, and high ridges along the diagonals run into the sea, so no forest can be seen or reached from the next except through the hub. Players slide off anything steeper than 45 degrees, and each forest's enemies only chase players in their own forest or the hub.
  - Joining players receive the seed from the host and build the same world; a checksum confirms the trees match.
  - The pause menu shows the seed.
- **Playing together through Steam:** friends join through Steam lobbies and Steam's relay network, so nobody types an address or opens a port, and nobody sees another player's IP (direct peer to peer is switched off).
  - Code: `NetworkBootstrap`, `SteamPlatform`, `SteamLobby`, and the vendored FishySteamworks transport.
  - Join codes (2026-10-04): every hosted Steam game gets a six character code (`JoinCode`, stored on a public Steam lobby, `SteamLobby.JoinByCode`). Friends type it on Join Game; the host sees "Name wants to join" and presses Y or N, or on a pad Start and then Let in or Turn away in the pause menu (`JoinApproval`, a FishNet authenticator; unanswered after 60 s means no). Without Steam (dev, LAN, tests) everyone is let in; test flags `-chopperRequireApproval`, `-chopperTestAnswer yes|no`, `-chopperAutoApprove`. The code is on the pause menu (Copy join code) and shown as a note.
  - Join Game also lists Steam friends who are in a world; the pause menu has Invite friends; Steam's own Join Game and invites also work (all still wait for the host's approval).
  - Development uses Valve's test app id 480 (`steam_appid.txt`).
  - Without Steam, solo play still works; tests use the local UDP transport.
  - Command-line local co-op runs can add repeatable outbound latency, jitter and packet loss (`-chopperLatency`, `-chopperJitter`, `-chopperPacketLoss`) to measure high-ping behavior without affecting normal play.
  - **Interest management:** enemies are only sent to players within 120 m and wood piles within 80 m (a FishNet `NetworkObserver` with a distance condition on each prefab, assets in `Config/Observers`, set up by `MeadowBuilder.AddDistanceObserver`). An enemy's hits, swings, slams and roots go only to those players too. On the managers every client observes, cosmetic effects (a tree shaking, saplings sprouting, a fish school) go as TargetRpcs to the players near the spot (`NearbyClients`). Anything that changes what a client knows (a tree felled, regrowth, announcements, the clock) stays global. The host saves every wood pile, seen or not.
  - **Felled trees on clients:** every peer keeps its own record of which trees are down (`TreeManager._clientFelled`, filled by the felled, load and late join RPCs, even when they arrive before the world is built). The owner's swing prediction reads it (`ClientFindTree`), so a joined player no longer treats a stump as a standing tree.
- **Saving:** each world is a file in the game's Saves folder (`World/Saving`: `WorldSave`, `SaveFiles`, `SaveSystem`, `WorldSaver`).
  - It saves at every dawn, from the pause menu, and when the host quits.
  - The file holds the clock, hut and walls, felled trees, saplings and bosses, the felled groves and an awake warden, wood piles, and crypt state.
  - Each player's record is keyed by a profile id kept on their PC (`ProfileId`, `PlayerIdentity`), so joining players get their things back too.
  - The start screen has Continue and Load Game; a loading screen covers the world build (`LoadingScreen`).
- **Water:** the pond, marsh pools, forest lakes and the sea use `Chopper/Water` (`Art/Shaders/ChopperWater.shader`). It reads the camera's depth and opaque textures (on in both pipeline assets): clear shallows that deepen to the water's own color, a soft shore fade with a broken foam line, refraction of the bottom, sky reflection at glancing angles (the live ambient sky when the baked reflection is too dark), a sun glint, and two scrolling ripple maps plus a slow swell. The colors are set in `MeadowBuilder.CreateWaterMaterials` (batch: `Chopper.Editor.MeadowBuilder.RebuildWaterMaterials`) and per forest lake in `WorldBuilder.WaterMaterial`. The pond and lakes are round because their bowls are carved as circles. They are flat discs facing up (they were squashed cylinders whose underside showed as a grey wall from below), and the camera is kept 0.35 m above any water surface, so looking up while swimming no longer puts it under the water.
- **Swimming and Wet (Valheim style):** in deep water the player floats with the chest at the surface (feet 1.05 m under). Swimming is slow (2.4 m/s, no sprint) and every stroke costs stamina; out of stamina the player only paddles (`SwimRules`). Getting in above the knees, or standing in rain, makes the player Wet for 90 s (`WetRules`): 10 percent slower on land and 30 percent slower stamina return, shown as a small "Wet" square by the stamina bar.
- **The house (2026-10-05):** three stations, each a plain scene component the server checks by position (`Workbench`, `StorageChest`, `Bed`); interact at one opens it (`PlayerWood.StationHere`).
  - **Workbench** ("Workbench" over "[E] Use"): its own menu (`InventoryView` in bench mode): Craft, Upgrades, Forge, Repair. It closes when you walk away. Recipes live in `RecipeBook` (inputs, output, station); everything is made at a workbench except the bandage (`RecipeBook.StationAllows`). The Tab screen keeps Character, Bag, Craft (bandage only) and Fishing; the Bag makes a bandage from a herb or fiber.
  - **Storage chest:** one chest for all players (`SharedChest` on the hut, a SyncList of counts per `ItemKind`, server authoritative, saved as `WorldSave.Chest`). It holds every carried item (wood, herbs, fiber, bandages, torches, iron, shards, essence, fish). The menu (`ChestView`) is two slot grids; a click or A moves a whole stack. `PlayerItems` reads and changes any item by kind.
  - **Bed:** from dusk on, interact lies you down (`PlayerRest`): no moving, the screen darkens, "In bed 1/2". When every player is in bed the clock runs on to dawn (`DayNightCycle.ServerSleepToMorning`), so the night's enemies wither and the world saves as at any dawn. Allowed every night (the user chose the Valheim rule). Respawns are beside the bed.
  - **Tools wear out:** everyone starts with the axe and a rod. The axe loses a point per hit (400), the rod per catch and 3 for a snapped line (40) (`ToolRules`, `PlayerAxe.Durability`, `PlayerFishing.RodDurability`). A broken axe leaves fists, a broken rod cannot cast. Repaired at a workbench (10 wood; 4 wood and 2 fiber). Thin bars on hotbar slots 1 and 4, no numbers. Saved as wear (`AxeWear`, `RodWear`), so older saves load whole tools. The axe no longer lies on the bench and is not taken from anywhere.
- **Building (Valheim style v1, 2026-10-05):** the hammer (key 6, or its hotbar slot on a pad; hotbar slot 6). Pieces are `PieceDefinition` assets in a `PieceCatalog` (made by `Editor/PieceBuilder` during Rebuild Network Scenes): wood floor, wall, doorway, roof (45 degrees), stairs, workbench, standing torch, campfire, from the pixel pack. A tinted copy follows the crosshair on the 2 m grid (`BuildRules`: cells, edges, a fine grid for free pieces, quarter turns); Block opens the piece list (`BuildMenuView`), Attack places, Repair or the wheel turns, Interact takes a piece down for its full cost. The server checks range (8 m), a workbench within 20 m (a workbench can go anywhere), cost, overlap with pieces and the world, support (nothing floats) and slope (`BuildManager`). Pieces are one SyncList of small structs (no network objects); every peer, the server included, keeps scene copies with colliders and NavMesh carving. Enemies hit the nearest piece in their way (`TreantAI`), and a piece at zero is gone; the Repair key at a workbench mends nearby pieces for wood. Saved as `WorldSave.Pieces`. Support (2026-10-05): every piece counts its steps to one on the ground (`BuildRules.SupportSteps`, at most 8, enough for two storeys and a roof); a piece too far away is refused ("Too far from the ground"), and when a piece is taken down or broken, whatever it alone held up collapses (its wood to whoever took the support away; nothing if an enemy broke it). Taking a piece down refunds by how whole it is, so refund plus repair is always the full cost. Near a placed piece, the ghost joins corner to corner (`BuildRules.SnapMoves`, `BuildManager.SnapToPieces`): the nearest join within 1.25 m that does not overlap, searching 6 m and trying at most 12. Not yet: the hammer model in the hand, distance culling of pieces, doors that open, player chests.
- **Mining (2026-10-06):** a pastime like fishing, with little weight on the rest of the game. One mine per forest, cut into a ridge (`WorldGen.Mines`, `MinePlan`, `MineWorld`, `MineManager`): a timber portal, then chambers, veins, lanterns and rails far below. The pickaxe (CC0, `OGA_LowPolyTools`, muted) is on the hotbar and swings on the vein's glint (`MiningRules`, `PlayerMining`): Clean, Good or Rough, one fading word. Eighteen finds (ore, stones, fossils, gems) are carried, chested and saved after the fish. A glowworm is bait that favors rarer fish; a catch sometimes brings up a geode or an old pick head, which makes the glow easier to catch. The Tab screen has a Mining page. Tour: `-chopperMine`.
- **Campfires:** two in the hut yard (south side, between the torches) and one on the pond bank facing the hut, where swimmers come out (`Campfire`, prefab built by `MeadowBuilder.BuildCampfirePrefab`, placed by `WorldBuilder` with the grass cleared under them). Standing within 4 m dries a Wet player nine times faster (about 10 s).
- **Grass:** a dense carpet drawn with GPU instancing (`GrassCarpet` plus the `Chopper/Grass` shader). It sways in wind gusts and parts around players and enemies.
- **Falling trees:** the first large tree a player fells drops toward that player as an early danger lesson. After that it works like Valheim: nothing tilts a tree to hint the fall (the oak bend is in the pixel art), a tree falls away from the last blow (within 25 degrees), and on a slope it comes down the hill whichever side it was cut from, so a tree chopped from below can come back down on the chopper (`TreeFallRules`).
- **Dying:** a large tree that lands on you kills you outright unless you wear Sturdy gear (`CrushRules`; blocking and wood armor do not help). The death screen names the cause. Carried wood is left in a pile where you fell (`WoodPile`); walk back and press interact to take it.
- **Crypts:** three seeded crypt entrances per world, one each in three different forests (`CryptPlan`, `CryptWorld`, `CryptManager`, `PlayerCrypt`).
  - The door takes you down to generated stone rooms 200 m under the entrance, lit by lanterns. Tier 1 to 3 sets the rooms, guardians and loot.
  - Guardians (treants of the crypt's element) wake on the first visit and stay through dawn.
  - Chests hold crypt iron and rune shards, looted once per player. The deepest room is the vault with a grand chest.
  - Underground there is no sun, sky sounds, grass or water, and the fog is dark.
  - At the workbench, iron buys **Sturdy gear** (survive a falling tree) and shards buy the **Rune axe** (more damage).
  - **Arena:** an archway in each vault leads 30 m down to an arena hall (`ArenaManager`).
    - Three waves of the crypt's guardians come first (more for later waves and more players), then the crypt's boss.
    - The boss drops essence of the crypt's element plus shards and iron, for everyone in the arena.
    - The arena resets if everyone leaves or falls, and stays quiet once won (saved).
  - **Elemental wards** (workbench, from essence): 25% then 50% less damage from enemies of that element.
- **Grove wardens (zone bosses):** `EnemyDirector` remembers where each forest tree was felled (`ZoneBossRules`). From day 3, once 18 lie within 35 m of each other (50 percent more after each boss beaten), the warden of that grove rises in the middle of them, at once, awake.
  - Phase 1: swings and ground slams.
  - Phase 2 (below two thirds): roots burst under every player in the grove after a short warning, and it calls guards (2 plus the player count). While a guard stands, its bark glows and only a quarter of each blow gets through.
  - Phase 3 (below one third): enraged, larger, faster, slams and swings more often, and calls a few more guards.
  - It and its guards are leashed to the grove (45 m): they never go for the hut, survive the dawn, and the warden heals and starts over if everyone leaves for 8 seconds. Health grows with the player count.
  - Beating it counts as a boss: a boon for everyone and 80 wood for the hut. The old boss saplings still load from old saves but are no longer planted.
- **Smooth movement:** the server's speed check (`PlayerMovementValidator`) allows sprinting plus sliding down a slope too steep to stand on (it used to snap sprinting players back near every mountainside, a freeze and a teleport), and it never checks the host's own player.
  - It also limits rising speed (a jump, or sprinting up the steepest walkable slope; falling is never limited).
  - Known fast moves are explicit: the server grants a short burst when it accepts a roll or the combo finisher lunge (`ServerAllowBurst`). Every server-side move of a player (respawn, crypt doors, arenas, loading a saved spot, the console's tp) goes through `ServerNotifyTeleport`.
  - Free flight is host only; the dev tools refuse it for joined players.
  - Every correction is logged as `[MovementCheck] Correction N` with the reason (horizontal speed, vertical speed, or ignored the last correction), the measured speed, the limit and any burst. `-chopperMoveTest` sprints a virtual pad round the hub, the ridges and the pond and counts teleports, freezes and snap backs (all 0, as host and as a client at 75 ms).
- **Tutorial (the practice yard):** Tutorial on the start screen hosts a fixed world (seed 2026) that is never saved, opens no Steam lobby and stays at day (`TutorialSession`, `TutorialYard`, `TutorialStations`). Every player gets the axe, a rod, torches, bandages, herbs, fiber, iron, shards and wood, the hut is repaired, and eight signs round the hub explain one thing each in the player's own keys: welcome, chopping, fighting (a soft hitting training tree that comes back after it falls), herbs and ferns, the workbench and repairs, torches and bandages, fishing at the pond, crypts. Leave from the pause menu. Tour: `-chopperHost -chopperTutorial -chopperTutorialTour`.
- **Signs:** text is never drawn on a sign; walk up and interact to read it in a panel (`Sign`). The signpost by the hut holds the guide. Player-written signs will use the same component once building pieces exist.
- **Minimal HUD:** no announcements in the middle of the screen and no objective text on the HUD (the current goal is at the top of the Tab screen). Prompts are Valheim's (2026-10-04, at the user's request after a Valheim screenshot): just right of the crosshair, what it is on the first line, then the key in yellow and what it does ("Healing herb" over "[E] Pick"; the hut shows "[E] Store wood" and "[R] Repair"). Built and cached in `InputHints.Prompt`. Notes on the left are only for things the screen does not show already. No toasts for parries or repairs (sparks and sounds instead), and fishing shows no words at all (see Fishing). Music and asset credits are on the start screen's Credits page.
- **Low health:** the screen edges shade a faint, steady red below 30 percent health that eases in and out. No flare on hits and no heartbeat pulse (the user found the pulsing jarring, 2026-10-05); the character's red tint shows a hit.
- **Pickup cards:** wood, herbs, fiber, bandages, crypt iron and rune shards show a card on the left of the screen with an icon, the amount and how many you carry (`PickupFeed`, `PickupFeedView`). Icons are game-icons.net silhouettes (`HotbarIcons`, listed in `GameIcons_net/SOURCES.md`), tinted in the menus; the bag, the build list and the map use them too. A dropped herb or fern is a 0.28 m sprig of the same model. Picking up works like Valheim: one press of interact on a herb, a fern or a fallen branch knocks it loose, it pops out onto the ground (`GroundItem`, spawned by `EnemyDirector.ServerDropItem`, seen within 80 m), and anyone who walks within 1.6 m with room for it takes it (a full pack leaves it lying there; it rots after 10 minutes). A dead player's wood pile is still emptied with a press. There are no holds in play at all (repair is a press too). Menu hold buttons (delete a world) show a thin line along their bottom edge.
- **Nothing purely decorative:** trees (wood), rocks (obstacles, stone later), red herbs and ferns (fiber; both make bandages), and fallen branches (+1 wood each, no punching needed) are all there is.
- **The opening loop:** wake up outside with the axe and a rod, gather wood (branches, 18 of them lying around the broken hut, or chopping), repair the hut, read the notice board.
- **Night creatures:** an awake forest enemy leaves the sapling pine and stands up as a wood creature (`GolemSetup`: the base body in the pine's bark, that element's pine foliage as a crown, on the animation rig). It walks, punches, flinches and falls. A stand-in until the Elderwood Guardian is imported (`requirements.md`). Crypt and arena guardians stay the draugr. Saplings and sleeping bosses stay pines.
- **Combat:**
  - Axe combo, fists, dodge roll.
  - **Axe block:** both hands on the axe, handle across the chest. There is no shield any more; it never sat in the hand.
  - Parry, enemy wind ups and stagger.
- **Pad hotbar (2026-10-05):** like other survival games: D-pad left and right move a gold cursor along the hotbar (wrapping), D-pad up uses the slot under it (takes it out, or puts it away if it is out). No chords, no radial menu. `PadHotbar` reads it; the six item actions have the pad path `Keybinds.HotbarPath` ("Hotbar", not rebindable), so every `Keybinds.WasPressed(KeyAction.Axe)` style call works unchanged. Old saved pad binds for items, the D-pad or LB chords fall back to the defaults. The keyboard keeps 1 to 6.
- **Unequip (2026-10-05):** H on the keyboard, R3 on a pad (Valheim's hide): puts the axe on the back, puts out the torch (what is left of it is kept), reels the rod in and puts the hammer away. Pressed again with empty hands, the axe comes back out. Crouch moved from R3 to LB on a pad; a saved crouch still on R3 moves to LB once (`Keybinds.MovePadCrouchOffR3`).
- **Chopping (2026-10-05):** a tree holds chop points (`ChopRules`, `TreeHitTracker`): 4, 6 and 9 plain axe hits for small, medium and large (was 1, 2 and 3). A swing takes off points by the axe's power (`PlayerUpgrades.AxeDamageMultiplier`: level, boons, runes), so a level 3 axe fells a large tree in 5 swings instead of 9; fist punches add up to one plain hit. Wood per tree went up by half (3, 6, 12) so the slower felling is not three times the grind. Enemy health is separate now (`GameConfig.GetTreantHealth`, still 1, 2, 3). Skills (logging speed) will multiply the same power.
- **Weather (checked 2026-10-05, `-chopperWeather`, 15 checks):** sun, cloud and rain. The wind picks up with the weather (`WeatherLook.Wind`: 1, 1.4, 2) and drives the grass sway through the global `_ChopperWind`; the rain slants along the same `WeatherRules.WindDirection`. Raindrops collide with roofs and the ground (exact collision; the voxel modes let drops through the hut roof), and nobody under a roof gets wet (`WeatherSystem.Sheltered`, a ray up from the head). Rain costs about 0.8 ms of CPU. The forest air (ambient particles setting) runs in all four forests.
- **Bosses (2026-10-05):** there is no separate warden creature; forest bosses (the grove boss and a grown boss tree) are the wood creature at about 11 m, checked in `-chopperCombat` (shots 7 and 8). The creature's bark now takes only 15 percent of the element glow and the crown 45 percent: the full glow had flattened it into a one-color silhouette. The crypt arena boss is 3.8 m (was 3.1) and carries a point light in its element's color so it reads in the dark arena. Real boss models: `requirements.md` 2b2.
- **Axe colors:** the axe's parts are colored by their materials' base colors (the textured axe: by material name, Metal or Wood). `PlayerBuffs.RefreshAxeColor` used to set white on the whole renderer, which painted the axe white; now each part keeps its color and only the grey metal head takes an element tint.
- **Camera zoom:** scroll wheel; on a pad D-pad down steps out, and from the farthest back to the closest.
- **Hands:** key 1 (on a pad the axe slot of the hotbar) draws the axe or puts it across the back; holstered, the player fights with fists, like Valheim's unarmed (`PlayerAxe.IsDrawn`, synced through the server). Anything in hand (the axe drawn, the torch lit) makes the player run 8 percent slower (`CarryRules`). Every handle sits in the hollow of the fist, found each frame from the curled finger bones (`HandGrip`); the axe's grip point used to sit off the handle (it was taken from the middle of the axe's bounding box, which is in the blade), so the axe hung in the air beside the fist. `CharacterSetup.MeasureAxe` now takes the grip from the handle's own center line.
- **The axe (2026-10-06):** a woodsman's axe, "Stylized Woodcutting Axe" by Price (OpenGameArt, CC0, `ThirdParty/OGA_WoodcuttingAxe`), converted from its .blend to 0.8 m with the head and handle as two materials (`WoodcuttingAxe_Metal`, `WoodcuttingAxe_Wood`); it replaced the Quaternius battle axe. Playtesters saw the old axe flip flop while running: the carry pose re-solved a forearm roll every frame, and when the swinging forearm passed the wanted line the roll jumped (up to 104 degrees in one frame). Now the carry turn is solved in the hand's own frame only while Chopper stands still and is held while he moves (`PlayerAxe.ApplyCarry`), so the axe rides with the arm swing. `-chopperAxeRun` measures it: 0 jumps over 25 degrees in a frame walking or sprinting (largest 10 degrees).
- **Torch:** a woodsman's torch built by `CharacterSetup.AttachTorch`: a tapered handle in the pixel pack's oak bark, a charred head with two rope bands, the pack's pixel flames (no smoke) and a flickering light. When lit, `HeldTorch` raises the left arm (elbow bent, fist in front at waist to chest height) and holds the torch nearly upright, so the flame is ahead of the chest. The guard and the rod take the arm back. A lantern was tried on 2026-10-04 and rejected: it did not fit the game. The yard torch posts burn with the same pixel flames.
- **Survival systems:** stamina, crafting at the workbench (torches; bandages from herbs and fiber anywhere), tool wear and repairs, axe, armor and hut upgrades, meals of fish for stamina, infinite lives with a 3 second respawn ("You died!" and nothing else on screen), day and night cycle, grove wardens and boons.
- **Interface:**
  - Start screen: logo plus Continue and Load Game (when worlds are saved), New Game, Join Game, Settings, Exit. New Game goes straight to the world name and seed, and Join Game to the friend list. The controls live in Settings.
  - Clean menus (soft panels, text buttons with a glow). The selected settings row lights up, so it is easy to follow with a controller.
  - Full keyboard and gamepad rebinding; PlayStation button names on a DualSense or DualShock.
  - A minimap (slim bronze ring, compass letters, house icon for the hut) and a world map that marks found crypts.
  - "Day N" shows for a few seconds at the start of each day, then fades. Nothing else marks the time: no night or dawn words and no countdown (removed 2026-10-06); the sun going down and coming up says it.
  - Defaults: FOV 80, camera zoomed in (2.5 m behind the player, nearly level; the wheel zooms out to 12 m); Settings General has Reset to defaults.
- **Audio:**
  - Effects only, no background ambience (no birds, crickets, wind or surf). Weather sounds come later with weather.
  - Every effect is a new CC0 recording (Kenney packs, Freesound), polished: rumble removed, silence trimmed, faded, and each family leveled. Sources are in `Resources/Audio/CREDITS.txt`.
  - Music by AlkaKrab under its royalty-free commercial license, credited on the start screen and the website. It follows the place and the hour (`MusicRules`, `AudioDirector`): the hub's calm day music, each forest's own, the calm night, and a fight theme while awake enemies are within 35 m. New tracks drop in by name (requirements.md section 5); until then each mood uses one of the six AlkaKrab tracks. The default music volume is a quarter (a saved one half, the old default, moves to it once).
  - Exactly one AudioListener at all times: `ListenerKeeper` on the NetworkManager switches its own on only while no other is active (the scene swap used to log "There are no audio listeners in the scene").
  - Footsteps change with the ground (grass, dirt, water, stone in crypts).
- **Builds:** Windows, Linux and macOS from this PC (`BuildTools`), zipped by `Tools/package_release.py`.
- **Website:** `Chopper/Website`, published only by hand (Actions, Chopper website, Run workflow). Six pictures, all from the current game (the hut picture was retaken on 2026-10-05 from the `-chopperHut` tour with the HUD hidden; tour pictures are fine when checked at full size). Sections: the game, home (workbench, chest, bed, building), crypts, fishing, playing together, status, credits. The shot list is `website-requirements.md`.

## Tests

- **EditMode:** house rules, the chest (fish and mining finds), building snap, mining, world generation (one mine in each forest), fishing, grove wardens, crypts, keybinds and the guard pose. The evening run on 2026-10-06 was 284 passed and 2 failed out of 286. The wall-snap check expected a cell center for a floor at the origin, and the chest count still ended at the last fish. Those two are fixed, and five tests were added (mining rules, and six seeds each getting four mines). Expect 291 once the editor is closed and they are run again.
- **Headless smoke test:** 323 checks of the whole game loop over the real networking, including a crypt visit, a grove warden fight, a save and load round trip, and the house (crafting at the bench, the chest, sleeping through the night, tool wear and repair, building pieces).
- **Co-op test:** a headless host and a joined client (`-chopperNetTest host` and `client`, with simulated latency) play a day and a night; the host checks that an enemy 600 m away is not sent to the client and one next to it is. Passes at 75 ms with jitter and 2 percent loss.
- **Movement test:** `-chopperMoveTest` as a joined client at 75 ms: 0 teleports, freezes and snap backs.
- **Swim tour:** `-chopperSwim` floats in the pond and the sea, swims, looks up, then dries by a campfire, logging `[Swim]` lines and taking pictures.
- **House tour:** `-chopperHouse` takes the workbench, chest and bed prompts and menus, the Tab bag and craft page, a small hut built from pieces (day and night) and the hammer's ghost and piece list, logging `[House]` lines.
- **Look tour:** `-chopperLook` takes five close pictures of Chopper from every side, then the axe drawn (close from the front and its side) and on the back, then the torch lit by day and at night. Check held items from the front: side views hid the floating axe.
- **Axe run check:** `-chopperAxeRun` draws the axe, walks then sprints, and logs `[AxeRun]` with any frame where the axe turns more than 25 degrees (a flip), plus eight pictures.
- **Pickup tour:** `-chopperPickup` shows the prompt, a herb popping loose and being taken.

## Known problems and gaps

- **Combat:** enemies fight as a group (CombatCircle): two attack turns per player, the rest circle on a ring round the player's sides and back, and each backs off after its swing. Forest enemies keep to their own forest and the hub.
- **Element stones:** permanent axe enchantments forged from arena essence and rune shards. Arenas return each dawn.
- **Fishing:** from any shore (hub pond, marsh, forest lakes, the sea), New World plus Ocarina of Time. A cast lands near a small school of fish drawn as dark shapes on the water (`LureRules`, `FishSchoolView`). Tap attack to twitch the lure, hold to reel it slowly; fish that notice it follow, and the keenest bites. Twitching too fast spooks them, a still lure is taken only late, and a lure reeled home ends the cast. Then the New World fight: strike, reel against line tension. Almost no words on screen: no prompts, no BITE, no zone or distance labels; only the single fading words below. A bite pulls the float right under with a splash; during the fight the only overlay is an unlabeled tension bar (green, orange, red with marks); a catch shows as a pickup card. Every fish has a weight (heavier ones fight harder), and the heaviest of each kind is saved as a record and shown on the Tab fishing page. The server runs the school and decides every bite. Each water has its own fish, and rain, night, and dawn or dusk add more. Rods and lures improve from arena bosses; a fish eaten (key 5) is a meal: extra stamina for 5 minutes (`FoodRules`, a "Fed" square by the stamina bar), not health. One word pops up and fades at the moments that matter (Hooked!, Caught!, Snapped, Got away, Missed), New World style. Old saves still load: the first four kinds were packed into one number.
- **Character (2026-10-06):** the creator is back. Six outfits (Chopper's plaid is the default), hair, beard, hair color, shirt color and skin, on a turning preview, synced to every player (`PlayerAppearance`, saved as `Chopper.Appearance2`). Each outfit has its own cut of the body. The Mixamo monk is out. The axe sits at the side, head forward, and rides the arm while running (0 flips on `-chopperAxeRun`). Drawing and holstering reach over the shoulder. A proper block and chop clip are still wanted (`requirements.md`).
- **Enemies:** awake forest enemies are the wood-creature stand-in (`GolemSetup`) until the Elderwood Guardian arrives. Saplings stay pines. Crypt and arena guardians stay the draugr.
- **Unconfirmed licenses:** the two axe sound files. (The pixel pack is CC0, by Markus Darius on itch.io, settled 2026-10-06.)
- **Multiplayer gaps (from the readiness review):**
  - The world handshake only logs a tree checksum mismatch: there is no game version check, the client is not told or disconnected.
  - Finding the tree in a swing loops all ~1,400 trees (with a cheap distance skip); the spatial grid (plan T12) is not built.
  - The server accepts a swing up to 0.1 s before its cooldown ends; heavy jitter could still reject honest swings. Now that the client's chop or combo guess matches the server's, measure rejected swings at 125 ms before changing it.
  - Player swing, roll and parry or block outcome RPCs are still sent to everyone (players are few and always observed).
- **Not built yet:**
  - Every tree is its own GameObject (about 1,400); this needs instanced rendering before the world grows.
  - The NavMesh does not update when trees fall.
