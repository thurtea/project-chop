# Chopper: development summary

Last updated: 2026-10-02. Project: `E:\Projects\3D-Game\Chopper` (Unity 6000.5.10f1, URP, FishNet 4.7.3, Quaternius CC0 art).
Design plan: `Docs/CHOPPER_PLAN.md` (inside the project folder, next to this file). Playtest checklist: `playtest.md`. Style rule: no emojis and no em dashes anywhere (docs, code comments, commits, in-game text).

## What the game is

(Short version: chop by day, defend the hut behind a palisade by night, infinite lives.)

Chop trees by day. Hits knock saplings loose. At night the saplings wake up as tree enemies and walk to the hut and to players.
The hut sits in a safe hub. Four forests surround it: North Water, East Earth, South Fire, West Wind.
Killing an elemental enemy loads your axe with that element for 15 hits, and a charged axe does bonus damage to enemies that element beats.
Rarely a boss sapling drops. It roots, grows over 2 days, must be chopped down, and the boss rises the next night.

## What exists and works (all tested)

- Saplings and the element loop: a FELLED tree (by day) plants 1 to 2 (small), 2 to 3 (medium) or 3 to 4 (large) saplings (`GameConfig` ranges, scaled up a little with more players). Each forest rolls every sapling 50/50 from its table: the forest's own element (Water forest: Water) or a plain brown one; hub trees give plain only. The felling player gets a message ("3 saplings planted (2 Water, 1 plain)"), the ground bursts where they sprout, and the top bar counts "Saplings planted: N (E elemental)". At night the nightly mix decides which kind wakes first (`NightRules.WakeElementalNext`), so restless nights bring the elemental ones first and quiet nights the plain ones, while every enemy keeps the element it was planted with. Kill any elemental enemy (say Water) and the axe is enchanted for 15 hits: +50 percent on everything, and a further +50 percent against the element it beats (Water beats Fire), with a "Super effective" message. So the player kills the Water ones, then takes that enchantment to the Fire ones.
- Multiplayer test: `-chopperNetTest host` plus `-chopperNetTest client` (two processes, `-chopperAppearance` gives each its own outfit). Verified: the client sees the felled tree, planted saplings and their elements, the hut repair, the axe, night and awake enemies, damage, boons, both outfits, and dawn withering and regrowth.
- Healing herbs (`HerbManager`): 240 small red flowers grow around the world (fixed seed, same on every peer, never in water or on steep ground). Pick one with E when the prompt shows; the server marks it picked, everyone sees it vanish, late joiners are told, and they all grow back at dawn. Potions (formerly salves) are brewed at the workbench from 3 herbs (`PotionHerbCost`), not wood; torches still use wood. The inventory shows "Healing herbs n / 20". Instructions for running, testing and updating are in `RUNNING.md`.
- Combat (`PlayerCombat`, `CombatRules`): block (right mouse or LB: slows you, cuts damage 75 percent, parries when raised just before a hit, costs stamina, guard breaks at zero, shield on the left forearm) and dodge roll (Q or Alt, R3: fast dash with 0.4 s untouchable, 18 stamina, `PlayerMotor.BeginRoll`). The server decides every hit (`PlayerHealth.ServerDamage` goes through `ServerMitigate`). Enemies wind up for about 0.7 s before hitting (`TreantAI`, visible rear back), are stunned and shoved by any non-lethal hit (`Treant.ServerStagger`, big ones and bosses resist), flash and throw chips (`CombatFx`). Enemy damage 4 per size step every 2.2 s (was 8 every 1.5 s). Axe base damage 2 (`AxeBaseDamage`), and any charged (enchanted) axe does +50 percent to everything (`EnchantDamageBonus`) on top of the +50 percent element counter.
- Water (`WaterSurface`, `WaterSystem`, `MeadowBuilder.BuildWater`): translucent rippling surface that reaches out to where the terrain rises; depth is surface minus terrain. Wading slows, deep water (1.25 m) means swimming: buoyancy, swim animations, no sinking, no rolling or blocking. Splash particles on entry. All water sound was removed (synthesized splash was bad); real recordings are listed in `requirements.md`. IMPORTANT: every MonoBehaviour must be in a file named after its class; `WaterSurface` inside `WaterSystem.cs` made the player build crash on load ("level1 is corrupted").
- Particles: `ZoneAtmosphere` has layered per zone effects (embers, ash, smoke, mist, drizzle, spores, leaves, dust streaks, seeds, fireflies) but they are OFF by default (Settings, "Ambient particles") because they distracted. Combat and roll particles are always on.
- Look option: Settings "Look: Smooth or Pixel" (`UserSettings.PixelLook`, render scale 0.42 with point upscaling, player builds only).
- Developer tools (`PlayerDevTools`, `DevPanelView`): F1 opens a host only panel with skip to night or day, god mode (`PlayerCombat.God`: no damage, endless stamina), wood, axe, heal, repair hut, spawn enemies, spawn a boss, random boon. Joined players are refused by the server.
- World start: players wake up at a random spot (24 spread-out flat dry points in the hub and forests; friends who join wake within a few meters of the first player) and must walk back to the hut, guided by the objective text and minimap. The hut stands on a 4.5 m mound (`MoundHeight`) as a Medieval Village kit house (plaster walls, door, windows, tiled roof) inside a palisade of round bark logs (`BuildWalls`; `WallSegment` tints the logs; the gate still sinks when a player is near). Respawns are at a random point until the hut is repaired, then inside the yard.
- The opening loop: players start with bare fists. The hut starts broken (darker look, gate shut). Punching trees gives wood (a punch is 0.34 of a hit, so three punches per hit, `_fistDamageMultiplier`; fists never use or earn element charges). Depositing wood at the hut repairs it first (`_hutRepairWood`, 10), then the gate opens and respawns move inside. The axe waits on an `AxeStand` in the yard (hold interact to take it, `PlayerAxe.HasAxe`), next to an `InfoBoard` that opens the `HowToPlayView` (also opens when the axe is taken, and from the Esc menu). An objective line and context prompts in `GameUi` follow the order: fix the hut, take the axe, chop, defend. `Hut.IsRepaired`, `HutWalls` (gate lock), `PlayerSpawnPoint.Outside`.
- Nights differ: each night rolls an elemental share between 15 and 85 percent (`NightRules`, `EnemyDirector`), applied as saplings wake, and the banner says whether it is a restless or a quiet night.
- Boss milestone and boons: after day 3 and 40 felled trees (raised by 50 percent of the base per boss beaten; `BossRules`) a boss tree is planted in a random forest. Felling it queues the boss for the night. Killing the boss gives everyone one random permanent boon (`PlayerBoons`: axe damage, armor, torch burn time, salve healing or stamina, up to level 5). Which boon is a random pick for now; choosing one or a fixed order is a one line change in `EnemyDirector.ServerOnBossKilled`. Boons last for the session (there is no save system yet).
- Meadow world, Valheim meadows scale: one 640 m terrain with gentle broad hills, a pond, a beach and sea at the edge (was a mountain rim until 2026-10-02), an open hub meadow of 110 m radius around the hut (scattered trees, rocks, grass), and four forests beyond it (north Water, east Earth, south Fire, west Wind) with their own tree species, leaf tint, ground color, fog color and a glowing marker pillar. Dirt paths run from the hut gate into each forest. 1700 baked trees (small about 5 m, medium 9 m, large 14 m) as data with plain GameObject views. Decor is grass (bends around players) and rocks with colliders only: no flowers or bushes.
- Chopping: server resolves axe hits by tree id. Small, medium, large fall in 1, 2, 3 hits. Wood 2, 4, 8. Hold left click to keep chopping. Chop feel: wood chips, leaf burst, camera shake, procedural placeholder sounds. Movement has acceleration. Third person camera chases behind you while you walk forward.
- Wood and hut: carry cap 40. Press E near the hut to deposit wood into the shared store (hold E next to a red herb to pick it). Press R (RB on a controller) near the hut to repair the palisade (most damaged segments first) and then the hut, paid from the store and then from carried wood. The hut has health, a wood store and 3 upgrade levels and starts at full health.
- Palisade: a ring of 24 wall segments (radius 11 m) around the hut, each with its own health. Enemies hit the walls before the hut. A destroyed segment opens a gap in their path. The north segment is a gate that sinks while a player is near, so players walk in and out but enemies never get through.
- Look pass: muted earthy ground palette, tileable detailed ground textures with normal maps generated by `MeadowBuilder`, sky reflections nearly off (they made everything look like plastic and painted a pale rim on terrain), stronger sun and calmer ambient, SSAO on, and a post profile with lower saturation, cool shadows and warm highlights, film grain and a vignette (`LightingSetup`, `DayNightCycle`, `ZonePalette`). Tree leaf tints were toned down but keep Water blue, Fire red, Earth brown, Wind green.
- Character creator: `CharacterCatalog` (asset in `Resources`, built by `OutfitSetup` from the CC0 Quaternius Universal Base Characters and Modular Character Outfits packs in `Assets/ThirdParty`) lists outfits (Lumberjack, Woodsman, Scout, Ranger, Trapper), hairstyles, beard, and hair, shirt and skin colors. `CharacterOutfitter` binds those skinned meshes to the existing UAL skeleton by bone name (the rigs match), hides the mannequin mesh, and tints materials by role. The body is cut down to a head only (`Mesh_Head`) because the outfit covers the rest. `PlayerAppearance` syncs one packed int per player (owner sends its saved choice, `AppearanceData` in PlayerPrefs). The start screen has a Character panel with a rotating live preview (`CharacterPreviewStage`, `CharacterPreview.prefab`). Male body only for now. Generated assets (catalog, materials, head mesh, preview prefab) are created by `Rebuild Network Scenes`.
- Website: the `Website` folder in this project (plain HTML, CSS and screenshots, published to thurtea.com/chopper/ by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`). It is part of this repository, not a separate one.
- Builds: `BuildTools` builds Windows, Linux and macOS (universal) release players and a dev player; `Tools/package_release.py` zips them with `PLAYTEST_README.txt`; `.github/workflows/release.yml` builds all three on GitHub with GameCI and attaches zips to a tagged release (needs the Unity secrets, see the file). Only Windows is built here so far: the Linux and macOS Unity modules need an administrator install (see `requirements.md`).
- Start screen (`TitleScreen`, uGUI in the Boot scene): New Game (host), Character, Join Game (address field), Settings (volume, mouse sensitivity, field of view, Display fullscreen or windowed, saved), Exit. The builds open borderless fullscreen at the desktop resolution; `UserSettings.Fullscreen` switches to a window at 80 percent of the desktop. The editor no longer auto hosts by default (`Chopper > Play > Auto Host On Play` turns it on).
- Zone look: each forest has its own ground color, fog color and density, relief, flora mix, tree density and leaf color: Water blue, Fire red, Earth brown, Wind green (hub natural green), done by `TreeTint` (grey leaf textures tinted per zone through property blocks). Enemy trees use the same colors; plain ones are brown, no glow, and grant no axe charge. `ZoneAtmosphere` adds drifting particles per zone (embers, mist and fireflies, spores, blowing leaves). Terrain uses a matte material with no reflections and does not cast shadows on itself.
- Enemy charge: enemies farther than 60 m from their target run at 2x speed (`_treantChargeDistance`, `_treantChargeMultiplier`), so a night that starts in a far forest still reaches the fight. The smoke test checks that an enemy hurts a player standing outside the walls before any wall or the hut is damaged.
- Day and night: day 330 s, dusk 15 s, night 150 s, dawn 15 s (8.5 minutes). Lighting, sky and fog change by phase. Tests guard that the day is long enough to visit all four forests and the night is long enough for the farthest enemy to arrive.
- Saplings and enemies: every chop hit can drop saplings (0 to 1 small and medium, 0 to 2 large, scaled by player count). Saplings cannot be hit or destroyed by the axe; they wake up as enemies at night a few at a time up to a cap, walk on a NavMesh baked on the server, and go for the nearest standing player within 40 m first, then the palisade, then the hut. Size sets hits to kill (1, 2, 3) and damage. Sapling element comes from the forest spawn table (hub trees give neutral saplings).
- Element charges: one kill of an elemental enemy loads that element for 15 hits. Each hit on an enemy spends one charge. A charged element gives +50 percent damage against the enemy it beats (Water beats Fire, Fire beats Wind, Wind beats Earth, Earth beats Water). Charges reset at dawn. The axe head takes the color of the strongest charge. Setting `Charge Only On Effective Hits` in `SO_GameConfig` makes only useful hits spend a charge.
- Boss: 1 percent sapling roll, grows over 2 days, ignores the axe until grown, then 30 hits to fell. The boss rises at the next nightfall with a ground slam and drops 60 wood into the hut store.
- Player: health and infinite lives. Going down respawns you at the hut after 12 s (time the enemies spend on the walls); only the hut falling ends the run. A finite lives mode (three per night, out of lives waits for dawn, everyone out restarts the run) exists behind the `Finite Lives` switch in `SO_GameConfig`, off by default, for a future game mode. Dawn restores health, withers survivors, resets charges and regrows felled trees. Everyone starts with one torch and one healing salve.
- Upgrades: near the hut press 1 for the axe, 2 for armor, 3 for the hut. Three levels each, paid from the hut wood store. Axe gets faster and hits harder, armor reduces damage taken, hut gets more max health.
- Game over: hut at zero health restarts the run at day one and resets upgrades.
- Lighting: URP shadows out to 140 m with 4 cascades and MSAA, a global post processing volume (ACES tonemapping, bloom, color grading, vignette), trilight ambient, a sun that arcs across the day (warm and low at dawn and dusk, blue moon at night) and night grading of exposure, tint and bloom. Cameras have post processing and SMAA on. Set up by `LightingSetup` and `DayNightCycle`.
- Reactive grass: grass and flowers live under `Grass` with `GrassField`; players and awake enemies register in `GrassPushers` and plants lean away (up to 42 degrees) and spring back. Tunables are on the `GrassField` component.
- Controls and camera (PC Valheim scheme): WASD or arrow keys move relative to the camera, the mouse always steers the camera (cursor locked, Esc frees it), the scroll wheel zooms (2.5 to 12 m), and the camera trails smoothly behind the player (`Camera Follow Speed` in `SO_GameConfig`). The character turns to face its movement direction and faces the camera direction when it swings.
- Player character: the Quaternius Universal Animation Library mannequin with an Animator (idle, walk and jog blend by speed, jump, death when down, and a sword-attack chop on an upper body layer so the legs keep walking) and the Medieval Weapons axe in the right hand. Built by `CharacterSetup`; `PlayerAnimator` drives it on every client. `Chopper > Setup > Capture Player Poses` renders poses to PNG for checking the model without playing.
- Sprint and crouch: Shift toggles sprint (stops when you stop moving or crouch), Ctrl toggles crouch (slower, lower camera, smaller collider, crouch animations, quieter footsteps). Crouch is synced so other players see it. The server speed check allows the sprint speed.
- Sound: real axe recordings (`Assets/_Chopper/Resources/Audio/axe-impact.mp3` for chops and hits, `axe-slash.mp3` for swings, from the earlier Chopper project) plus synthesized placeholders in `ProceduralSfx` for everything else, played through a pooled `Sfx`: footsteps, axe swing, chop, tree fall, wood pickup and deposit, upgrade chime, player hurt, enemy growl and death, hut hit thump, night horn, dawn birds, plus ambient wind by day and crickets by night (`AudioDirector`).
- Night visibility: a big clock at the top of the screen (Day: night falls in, Dusk, Night: dawn in) and banners at dusk and nightfall.
- Interface (built in code with uGUI, `GameUi`): bottom left health and stamina bars plus axe element charge icons, bottom center hotbar (1 axe, 2 torch, 3 healing salve), top center day clock and hut health, pickup messages down the left, a round minimap bottom right with fog of war (north up, hut, forest markers, enemies, players), damage flash and a down screen. The top right corner is empty on purpose.
- Menus: Esc pause menu (Resume, Settings with volume, mouse sensitivity and field of view saved between sessions, Controls, Disconnect, Quit), Tab inventory and hut workbench, M world map. Menus free the cursor and block gameplay input but the world keeps running.
- Controller (Xbox): left stick move, right stick look, RT chop (hold), A jump, B toggle crouch, L3 sprint toggle, X deposit (hold), RB repair, LT torch, LB salve, Y inventory and workbench, Back map, Start menu, D-pad up and down zoom. Menus work with stick, D-pad, A and B. The HUD shows button names for whichever device you used last.
- Survival depth: stamina (140 max; sprinting drains 6 per second, jumps cost 8; swings cost 6 only when an awake enemy is in front, chopping trees is free; exhaustion lock), workbench at the hut where carried wood crafts torches (light the night, held in the left hand, 4 minute burn) and healing salves, and the axe, armor and hut upgrades are now bought there with buttons (the old 1 2 3 upgrade keys are gone).
- Camera collision: the camera stops in front of walls, trees and the hut instead of passing through them.
- Easier testing: pressing Play always starts Boot and shows the start screen (`Chopper > Play > Auto Host On Play` skips it; `Chopper > Play > Start Game` also works).
- Playable exe: `Chopper > Build > Windows Playtest Player` (batch: `-executeMethod Chopper.Editor.BuildTools.BuildPlaytestPlayer -buildOutput <folder>`) writes a release build; the current one is in `Builds\Release\windows\Chopper.exe` and `Builds\Release\Chopper-0.1.0-windows.zip` (git ignored).

## Crisp resolution and lighting pass (2026-10-01)

- Resolution: the pipeline already rendered at native scale with 4x MSAA and SMAA, so the work was exposing it and removing blur. A new Graphics tab in Settings (`SettingsView`, `UserSettings.ApplyGraphics`) sets render scale (50 to 150 percent, above 100 supersamples), anti-aliasing (Off, SMAA, MSAA 2x, 4x, 8x, MSAA 4x + SMAA which is the default), shadow quality (Low to Ultra: 1024 to 8192 map, 70 to 180 m), vsync (on by default) and the Pixel look toggle (off by default). Pipeline asset writes happen in players only (the editor guard stays), camera SMAA is applied to every game camera. Film grain was cut from 0.16 to 0.05 and contrast from 22 to 14.
- Lighting: shadow strength 0.85 on the sun (shade keeps color), day ambient raised (equator 0.68, ground 0.42), night made readable moonlight (moon 1.0, night ambient about 1.7 times brighter, exposure +0.15), saturation loss reduced, rock rim and hills warmed from grey to olive.
- Pack materials (`PixelPackSetup.BalanceMaterials`): the pack's textures and tints are very dark (the roof is about 4.5 percent albedo in linear), which made the house and trunks black. Dark tints are normalized and structures, trunks and furniture get a linear gain of 3.0, 2.5 and 1.8; emission is clamped to 2.4 (the lanterns were 11, a white square). `WallSegment` now tints per material by multiplying each material's own color, so wall damage keeps the balanced look.
- Dev tools: `-chopperLight` screenshot tour (morning, noon, late, dusk, night at yard, hub, earth, fire; jumps the clock with `DayNightCycle.ServerDebugSetClock`). Tests: 147 EditMode (5 new `GraphicsSettingsTests`), smoke test PASS.
- Not done or to judge: a real-hardware crispness check (render scale 150 and MSAA 8x performance), Windows display scaling in windowed mode, the lantern glass is now a flat light square (could use an emissive texture), the water surface still uses the old ripple material, the HUD font, the night is readable but still dark away from the torch by design.

## Controller menus, audio and the guard pose (2026-10-01)

- Controller navigation: the title screen and every in game menu work with a pad. `UiKit` buttons, tabs and sliders have a visible selected state (it was invisible before, so the D-pad seemed to do nothing), sliders use vertical-only navigation so left and right change the value, LB and RB switch the settings tabs (`SettingsPager`, with a hint line while a pad is connected), B goes back one level (title screen: `TitleScreen.HandlePadAndBack`; pause menu: `PauseMenuView.InSettings` and `Back`, Start always closes it), and the selection moves into a panel when it appears or if a mouse click cleared it. Verified by `-chopperPadTest` (24 checks: selection, moves, A, both bumpers, slider, B from every screen, character creator).
- Audio: measured with `AudioExport` (menu Chopper > Setup > Export Sounds, WAV files) and `Tools/analyze_sounds.py`. The old wind was the loudest sound in the game (-12 dB RMS, 462 clipped samples, all sub 200 Hz rumble) and most synthesized sounds were 5 to 10 dB louder than the real axe recordings. Now every synthesized sound is normalized to a target loudness in `ProceduralSfx` (`Levels`, about -22 dB for effects, -31 footsteps, -33 wind, -37 crickets) with a soft limiter instead of hard clipping. The wind is rebuilt: three layers (steady body, a whoosh band whose center follows the gust, leaf rustle in strong gusts) shaped by two slow gust signals, 24 s equal power loop, 12 dB swing between calm and gust. Crickets are softer with vibrato, footsteps are a soft thud with a little crunch.
- Volumes: master default 70 percent (was 80), new Effects volume (100 percent default) and Ambience volume (80 percent) sliders in Settings > General; `AudioDirector` reads them every frame; wind and cricket trims are gentle (0.9 day, 0.6 night, 0.8 crickets).
- Crosshair: the dot in the middle of the screen is removed.
- Shield guard: the library has no block clip, so the guard is a procedural pose (`GuardPose`, added to the model by `CharacterSetup`, driven by `PlayerAnimator`). Each arm bone is aimed along a direction in the character's frame (shield arm raised in front of the chest, axe arm drawn up with the axe tipped forward), the shoulders turn toward the shield side, and the whole spine is aimed at a fixed 8 degree lean (so the animator's own forward lean no longer stacks), the head stays level, all blended in 0.18 s and out in 0.14 s. The shield holder is rotated -90 degrees (`CharacterSetup.ShieldEuler`) so its face points forward and stands upright. While blocking, the body faces the camera but follows its movement direction up to 50 degrees (`PlayerMotor.BlockFacingYaw`), so sidestepping no longer skates.
- Tools: `Chopper > Setup > Capture Guard Pose` and `Capture Shield Trials` render the pose from several sides using the real animator state; `-chopperLight`, `-chopperCombat` (the tour now waits out the How to play screen before raising the guard) and `-chopperPadTest` are the runtime checks. Tests: 153 EditMode (6 new `GuardPoseTests`), smoke test PASS.
- Important: the development player shares its saved settings (PlayerPrefs, registry `HKCU\Software\DefaultCompany\Chopper`) with the real game. A test run that drove a slider lowered the saved field of view to 50; it was restored to 87 with `-chopperPadTest -chopperSetFov 87`. Any runtime test must restore what it touches.
- Not done: strafe and backward walk animations while blocking (the library has only forward clips), legs and knees in the guard, hit reactions while guarding, real recorded wind and footsteps (CC0 samples would beat any synthesis), a real pad test on hardware.

## Night themes

- Each night rolls an elemental share (15 to 85 percent) and a dominant element, picked among the elements that have saplings waiting (more saplings, more likely). The dominant element is most of the swarm, its counter (the element that beats it) is the second most common so the axe can always be charged on a kill, the rest are rare, plain trees fill the remaining share. Where you chop decides what grows, so a player who felled mostly in the Fire forest gets Fire nights.
- The dawn banner names the theme: "A Fire night. Fire trees swarm. Kill a Water tree to charge your axe against them."

## Combat combos and shield tiers (latest)

- Axe combo: swings at enemies or empty air chain into a three hit combo (slash, backhand, heavy finisher). Swing again within about a second of the last one to continue. The backhand is the slash animation played backwards; the finisher uses the cross punch body motion, does 1.6x damage, lunges forward, staggers the enemy and takes longer to recover from. Chopping a tree is always the plain chop and does not advance the combo. Bare fists alternate jab and cross.
- The combo step is decided on the server (damage) and mirrored on the owner (animation), see `ComboRules` and `PlayerAxe`.
