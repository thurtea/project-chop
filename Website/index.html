Game

Start beside a ruined hut. Repair it, gather supplies, and explore four forests: Water, Earth, Fire, and Wind. Each world grows from a seed, so friends can share the same map.

Trees you cut grow back, and the woods come after you at night. Stay close to home and the night stays quiet. Build floors, walls, roofs, stairs, torches, and campfires. Fish the ponds and the sea, mine the forests, and fight through crypts for gear.

One to four players, co-op. Host a world and share a six-character code, or invite Steam friends. Each player keeps their own gear. The host's world saves at dawn.
