## Decisions

- 2026-10-11: New Unity project lives in `Chopper/` inside this repo, on branch `rebuild/chopper-assets`. The old project at the repo root is left untouched; removing it needs the user's approval.
- 2026-10-11: The canonical axe is the double-headed axe of concept 1.7 (as the brief says). The single-bladed bear-head axe in concepts 3 and 1.5 is not modeled for now.
- 2026-10-11: "By hand in Blender" is done through Blender Python with explicit geometry, not AI image-to-3D.

- 2026-10-11 (Week 2 start): One 2048 x 2048 atlas shared by all modules and gear; materials stay M_Chopper and M_Chopper_Gear (both sampling the atlas). Final look uses the atlas, not vertex colours. Capsule radius stays 0.37 and axe blades stay front and back until the user decides.

## Open issues

- Gray-box look, to fix in the Week 2 or 3 polish (not blocking the rig): beard sticks out about 9 cm from the face and reads as a stepped block, not a round V; back hair reads as a busy cluster in side view; torso is a straight box without the concept's broader chest; boot wrap bands read as stacked rings; axe blade throat shallower than concept 1.7 and axe sits at 986 of 1,000 tris; tiny fold in the shield rim at the top-left inner corner.
- From W1-04: Toes bone is not animated (no edge loop at the ball of the foot); walk is 0.75 m/s in place, so Unity must move the character at that speed; axe blades face front and back (chopping direction), not outward as in concept 1.5; `Shield_Back` placed by calculation only, not clip-checked; walk bob only 1.5 cm; beard overlaps the chest at rest, head turns kept to 3 degrees or less.
